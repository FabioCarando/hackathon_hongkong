## Judging criteria

The HK criteria **have not been published**. The Cyprus edition of iFX Hack (June 2026, same organiser, also powered by AWS) judged on the list below. Assume it will be reused until told otherwise.

| # | Criterion | What the jury is likely looking for |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Innovation** | A fresh angle on a known problem, not a thin wrapper around a chatbot |
| 2 | **Technical execution** | A sound architecture, real use of AI and AWS, and engineering choices you can defend |
| 3 | **Functionality** | It works live, end to end, on stage |
| 4 | **Problem-solving approach** | A clearly framed problem, a named user, and a solution that fits the problem |
| 5 | **Industry impact** | A buyer exists, the value can be measured, and it could realistically be adopted |

Implicit criteria (inferred, not stated):
- **AWS is the title sponsor**, so AWS services should be visibly part of the architecture.
- **The audience at the Final is the online trading industry**: brokers, liquidity providers, payment providers, prop firms, regtech vendors. They judge as potential buyers.

## What separates the Top 7 from the rest

1. **A named buyer and a painful number.** For example, "HK brokers lose X to account-takeover fraud" or "a compliance analyst spends 40 min per alert; we cut it to 4."
2. **A live demo on real data, with a backup video.** The Final is on a noisy expo stage over venue wifi, so always have a recorded fallback.
3. **A 10-second "wow" moment.** One visual moment the jury will remember.
4. **HK/APAC localisation.** Name the SFC or HKMA, show Chinese/Cantonese support, and mention cross-border flows, virtual-asset rules or FPS.
5. **Guardrails and explainability.** Every output shows its source, its confidence and a step where a human decides. Finance judges punish black boxes.
6. **Scope ruthlessly on 4 Oct.** Get one end-to-end flow working by about 17:00, then only polish. Teams that ship three half-done features lose.
