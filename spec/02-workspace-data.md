# 02 · Workspace and data

The demo runs on a fake but realistic finance workspace for **Lantau Peak Family Office Ltd (大嶼峰家族辦公室有限公司)**, a Hong Kong single-family office. It's generated by `scripts/generate_workspace.py` (deterministic, re-runnable) into `workspace/`. Never hand-edit `workspace/`. Change the generator and rerun it:

```bash
uv run --with openpyxl --with fpdf2 --with pymupdf python scripts/generate_workspace.py
```

All names, banks, addresses and domains are fictional.

## The company

- HK single-family office for the **Cheung family** (assets held for the Cheung Family Trust). About US$350M: a listed portfolio at Lion Rock Private Bank (custodian), four private fund commitments, two rental flats in HK, an art collection, and the family's expenses (office and household staff, school fees, travel and aviation charter, insurance).
- Functional currency HKD, also USD and RMB. FY = calendar year. FX in `sheets/fx_rates.xlsx` (about USD 7.80, RMB 1.08).
- "Today" in the data = **2026-10-05**. The September close is in progress.
- People:
  - **Victoria Cheung**: principal and family member. Approves anything over **HK$500,000**.
  - **Raymond Ho**: CFO, the buyer.
  - **Grace Lam**: fund accountant, the main user.
  - **Jason Yip**: accounts clerk, does the intake.
- Company memory: `workspace/COMPANY.md` (profile, people, approval rule, account conventions, counterparties with known domains, policies, forecast assumptions). **The app reads the company name, people, email domain, approval rule, account conventions and the forecast named range from this file.** Nothing company-specific is hardcoded in `app/`.

### Document kinds and account codes

Payable documents come in three kinds: `capital_call`, `fee_notice` and `invoice`. The LLM classifies the kind. The account code comes from COMPANY.md conventions for calls (150) and fee notices (455), and otherwise from the counterparty's usual account in the register.

### `sources.json` schema (per-cell provenance, used by the app too)

```json
{
  "file": "sheets/forecast_2027_2028.xlsx",
  "sheet": "Forecast",
  "cell": "C5:Z5",
  "value": 95000,
  "sources": [{"doc": "COMPANY.md", "page": null, "quote": "Rental income for Flat 12A Repulse Bay ... assumed flat at HK$95,000/month ..."}],
  "reason": "Repulse Bay rent held flat at the current lease rate until the lease expiring 31 Dec 2026 is renewed.",
  "commit": "17b9fd46e3acb1efe5b99a12b7c1a315ba4f5193"
}
```

`cell` can be a single cell or a range. The app appends to this list on every accepted change and fills in `commit` after committing.

## Data rules

- Amounts in the register match the PDFs exactly. `amount_hkd = amount × fx_rate` of the document month.
- Held documents are still written to the register with `status = "HELD"` so the document is traceable, but they are never `"Entered"` (payable) until a human approves. This matches `tasks[1]`.
- New register rows are appended after the last used row (row 11 onward).
- Accepting a capital call does **not** yet update `commitments.xlsx` (next step). Ask combines the sheet with processed documents.
